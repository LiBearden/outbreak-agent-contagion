----- question -----
Interpret the results of the Outbreak study. Use only the packet: the frozen preregistration (PREREG.md), the design spec (SPEC.md), the README, and the frozen analysis output tables (the CSV files). Write your own reading of what the data show.

Use exactly these five headings, in this order:

Rules: plain text, no tables, at most 900 words in total. Every number you state must appear in, or be computed directly from, a packet table; name the table. Do not say who or what you are. End with one line: "Confidence: <a number from 0 to 1>" for your bottom line.

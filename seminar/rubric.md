# Rubric for the blind judge (deputy PI)

Fixed on 2026-11-08, before any take was written. The judge sees the packet, this rubric and the anonymized takes (labels A, B, C, D) only.

Score each take from 1 to 5 on each criterion, with one or two sentences of reasons that cite the take's own words. Then rank all takes and name one winner. A tie needs a stated reason.

1. **Fidelity (weight 3).** Check at least five stated numbers per take against the named packet tables. Any number that is wrong, invented or attributed to the wrong table caps this score at 2. Quote each number you checked and whether it matched.
2. **Decision rules (weight 3).** H3 and H5 are read by the rules the prereg states (the agreement band and the too-few-events rule for H3, and the supported and refuted-at-0.3 rule for H5). Confirmatory and secondary roles are kept apart, and H4 is called untestable, not negative.
3. **Calibration (weight 2).** Claims stay inside the design (one model, 8 agents, text only, an inert marker, the placebo baseline, controls that may sit at ceiling), and uncertainty is stated where the intervals call for it. Overclaiming costs more than caution.
4. **Insight (weight 1).** The take finds what matters most in these results, including implications the tables support but do not state.
5. **Limitations (weight 1).** Threats are specific to this study and material, not boilerplate.

Report: a score table by label, the weighted total, the ranking, the winner, and the numbers you checked. Refer to takes by label only. Do not guess who wrote a take. A take may be written by a person, and length or style is not evidence of quality.
